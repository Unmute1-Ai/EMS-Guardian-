Unmute1ai builds tools for the moments systems weren't designed for — a responder's voice for patients who can't speak, and assessments that publish their evidence instead of asking for trust.

What we make.

Two products, each with its own page and its own job — neither is a subsection of the other. Pick a door.

Field communication

Argus Responder

A voice for patients who sign, speak another language, or can't speak at all. Argus reads ASL, translates in real time, and drafts the handoff — even with no signal.

U1 AccessGuard
