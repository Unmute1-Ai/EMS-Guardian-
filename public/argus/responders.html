Why I made this

I worked 911 EMS in New York City. I know how it feels to have that call. The one that comes home with you. In that moment you don't need a form or a lecture. You need someone to talk to, so you can get to the next shift, and the next call.

Too many of us carry a weight we were never meant to hold alone. I built this so no one has to.

Matthew Sacks · Former 911 EMS, New York City · Founder, Unmute1ai
U1Voice

A voice that listens after the hard ones.

U1Voice is a calm voice companion inside Argus. Talk through a call in your own words, on your own time. It listens, reflects back, and helps you find the next step, whether that's rest, a teammate, or a peer-support line.

  • Private by design: conversations stay on your device
  • No forms and no typing. Just talk.
  • Knows the job: shift work, tones, the calls that stick
  • Points you to real people when you need more than a voice

U1Voice is in development. It is a companion, not a counselor or a crisis service.
