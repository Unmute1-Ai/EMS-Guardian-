    Disconnected operations

    Basements. Rural roads. Disaster zones. The signal drops; Argus doesn't.

    After one online load, the app and its hand-tracking engine are stored on the device. When the network goes, voice and reporting switch to on-device fallbacks. Patient data is never stored in the offline cache.
